PHOTOSYNTHESIS IN HIGHER PLANTS - my notes (Class 11 Biology)

What it is
Photosynthesis makes glucose from carbon dioxide and water using light energy. Oxygen is released.
Overall: 6CO2 + 12H2O --(light, chlorophyll)--> C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O
The oxygen released comes from WATER, not from CO2 (proved using O-18 labelled water, Ruben and Kamen).

Where it happens
In chloroplasts. Thylakoid membranes (stacked into grana) = light reaction. Stroma = dark reaction (Calvin cycle).
Pigments: chlorophyll a (main pigment, reaction centre), chlorophyll b, carotenoids (accessory pigments - absorb other wavelengths and protect from photo-oxidation).
Chlorophyll absorbs mainly blue and red light. It reflects green, which is why leaves look green.

Light reaction (needs light)
- Photosystem II (P680) and Photosystem I (P700).
- Photolysis of water at PS II: 2H2O -> 4H+ + O2 + 4e-. This replaces the electrons PS II lost.
- Electrons pass along the electron transport chain from PS II to PS I.
- Products: ATP (by chemiosmosis - H+ flows through ATP synthase) and NADPH.
- Non-cyclic photophosphorylation uses both PS I and PS II and gives ATP + NADPH + O2.
- Cyclic photophosphorylation uses only PS I and gives only ATP (no NADPH, no O2).

Dark reaction / Calvin cycle (does not need light directly, but needs ATP and NADPH from the light reaction)
1. Carboxylation: CO2 + RuBP -> 2 molecules of 3-PGA. Enzyme: RuBisCO (most abundant enzyme on Earth).
2. Reduction: 3-PGA -> G3P using ATP and NADPH.
3. Regeneration of RuBP using ATP.
To make 1 glucose: 6 turns of the cycle, 18 ATP and 12 NADPH.
"Dark reaction" is a misleading name - it happens in the day, because it needs the products of the light reaction.

C4 plants (maize, sugarcane)
- First stable product is a 4-carbon compound (oxaloacetic acid), not 3-PGA.
- Kranz anatomy: bundle sheath cells around the veins.
- PEP carboxylase fixes CO2 first in mesophyll cells. It does not react with O2.
- Advantage: almost no photorespiration, so better yield in hot, bright, dry places.

Photorespiration
- At high temperature and high O2, RuBisCO binds O2 instead of CO2.
- This wastes energy and releases CO2. No sugar, no ATP is made.
- C3 plants suffer from it; C4 plants avoid it by concentrating CO2 around RuBisCO.

Factors affecting rate
Light intensity, CO2 concentration, temperature, water.
Blackman's law of limiting factors: the rate is limited by the factor in shortest supply.
Example: on a bright day, adding more light won't help if CO2 is the limiting factor. Greenhouses add CO2 to raise yield.
